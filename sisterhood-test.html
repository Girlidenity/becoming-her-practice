The Woman I Choose to Become

Queen, can we talk about your future? Not the future someone else planned for you. Not the future you thought you were supposed to have. The future you want to create with the understanding you have today. If you stopped measuring your life against everyone else's expectations, what kind of woman would you choose to become? Not what would she own or look like. Who would she be? How would she treat herself and others? What values would guide her decisions?

When you imagine the woman you want to become, what qualities matter most to you?

Are those qualities based on your own values, or are you trying to become someone you believe others will approve of?

Name three qualities you want to practice more intentionally in your everyday life.

👑 Becoming Her begins with deciding what matters to you, not proving yourself to the world.

SPACE 26 / 36 · Becoming Her

Breaking the Cycle

Queen, have you ever promised yourself you would never do something again, only to find yourself repeating it? Maybe it was a relationship pattern, a reaction when you're upset, a habit that makes life harder, or a decision you make when you're afraid. Sometimes we recognize a cycle long before we understand how to change it. Change requires noticing what happens before the pattern begins, understanding what keeps it going, and finding another way to respond.

What pattern are you ready to interrupt because it no longer supports the life you want?

What situations, emotions, fears, or unmet needs tend to appear before that pattern repeats?

What is one realistic response you could practice the next time you notice it beginning?

👑 Breaking a cycle doesn't mean you'll never struggle again. It means you're learning to recognize when you have another choice.

SPACE 29 / 36 · Becoming Her

When Life Doesn’t Follow My Plan

Queen, can we talk about disappointment? Even when we make thoughtful decisions, life doesn't always cooperate. Opportunities fall through. Relationships change. Plans get delayed. We experience setbacks we didn't expect. Sometimes we question whether all the work we've done has mattered. Growth doesn't guarantee that everything will go according to plan. It can help us develop new ways to respond when it doesn't.

How do you usually respond when something important doesn't happen the way you hoped?

Do you give yourself room to feel disappointed, or do you immediately criticize yourself, give up, or pretend it doesn't matter?

What could help you respond to your next setback with both honesty and compassion?

👑 A setback can change your direction without taking away your ability to move forward.

SPACE 30 / 36 · Becoming Her

Learning to Trust My Own Voice

Queen, whose voice do you hear when you're making an important decision? Your mother's? Your partner's? Your friends'? The people who doubted you? The people you don't want to disappoint? Or your own? There's value in listening to people who care about us. But somewhere along the way, we also have to learn how to recognize our own values, needs, and judgment. That doesn't mean we'll always make the right decision. It means we're willing to take responsibility for the choices we make.

When was the last time you questioned a decision you felt good about because someone else disagreed?

Were their concerns helpful information, or did their disapproval cause you to doubt yourself without examining what you truly believed?

What questions can you ask yourself before your next important decision to make sure it reflects your values and circumstances?

👑 You can listen to wise advice without surrendering your ability to choose.

SPACE 31 / 36 · Becoming Her

The Courage to Choose Differently

Queen, can we talk about courage? Sometimes we imagine courage as doing something extraordinary. But courage can be much quieter than that. It can look like having an honest conversation, admitting you were wrong, asking for help, walking away from something that no longer serves you, or taking the first step toward something you've wanted for years. Sometimes courage even looks like admitting you're afraid. Being brave doesn't mean fear has disappeared. It means you're learning how to make thoughtful choices even when fear is present.

What change have you been considering but haven't yet felt ready to make?

What are you afraid might happen if you make that change? What support, information, or preparation might help you approach it safely?

What is one manageable step you can take toward that change without expecting yourself to accomplish everything at once?

👑 You can be afraid and still be capable of making a thoughtful decision.

The People Walking Beside Me

Queen, let's talk about your village. Who celebrates you when something good happens? Who can tell you the truth without making you feel small? Who respects your boundaries? Who checks on you when you're not the strong one? And who are you becoming in the lives of the people you love? Sometimes our experiences teach us to believe we must handle everything alone. But independence and isolation are not the same thing. You can be strong and still need connection.

Are your closest relationships built on mutual care, or do you often feel responsible for maintaining the connection by yourself?

What is one way you can nurture a healthy connection, ask for support, or begin building the kind of village you need?

SPACE 33 / 36 · Becoming Her

Making Peace With Change

Queen, have you noticed how much life can change? The people we thought would always be present may take different paths. The dreams we once held may no longer fit. Our bodies, responsibilities, relationships, and priorities can change. Sometimes we welcome those changes. Sometimes we grieve them. And sometimes we feel both emotions at once. Changing doesn't mean you've betrayed the person you used to be. You are allowed to outgrow a belief, reconsider a decision, or discover that something you once wanted is no longer what you need.

What change in your life have you struggled to accept?

What are you grieving, protecting, or afraid of losing as that change unfolds?

What could help you honor what mattered about the past while making room for your present reality?

👑 You can appreciate who you were without requiring yourself to remain that person forever.

SPACE 35 / 36 · Becoming Her

Defining Success for Myself

Queen, who taught you what a successful life should look like? Was it having a certain career, owning a home, being married, raising children, having money, or being admired? There's nothing wrong with wanting those things. But what happens when your life doesn't follow the picture you once imagined? Does that mean you've failed? Or could success deserve a definition that reflects your own values and circumstances? Success can include peace, integrity, healthy relationships, personal freedom, meaningful work, or the courage to begin again.

How have you measured your success, and whose expectations have influenced that definition?

What parts of your life feel meaningful to you even if they don't impress anyone else?
